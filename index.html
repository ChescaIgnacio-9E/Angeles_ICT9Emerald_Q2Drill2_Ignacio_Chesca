Home Page

Welcome to the O.B. Montessori Center! At OBMC,each child is nurtured in the holistic method of Dr. Maria Montessori. We blend tradition and innovation to prepare a child for life. Our mission is to transform our students into self-developing and productive individuals who can succeed, direct, and influence the future of an ever-changing world. We are a progressive pre-k to 12 co-educational school, offering a viable alternative to international schools. Explore our programs and discover the vibrant community that makes OBMC a unique place for a child to grow and develop.
